Demo overview

Three independent apps sharing one user-owned application space.

Food Tracker and Pain Tracker write their own records under separate app prefixes. Insight discovers their manifests, requests compatible read access, and correlates meals with later pain events.

How it works

A complete interoperability loop.

Food Tracker

Logs meals, ingredients, optional notes, and photos.

Writes
xyz.tinycloud.tinyapps.food/records/meals/
Manifest
xyz.tinycloud.tinyapps.food/manifest.json
Open Food Tracker

Pain Tracker

Logs severity, location, quality, duration, and notes.

Writes
xyz.tinycloud.tinyapps.pain/records/events/
Manifest
xyz.tinycloud.tinyapps.pain/manifest.json
Open Pain Tracker

Insight

Lists manifests, reads declared datasets, and shows patterns.

Reads
applications manifests and compatible records
Output
Meal and pain timing correlations, never diagnosis.
Open Insight
